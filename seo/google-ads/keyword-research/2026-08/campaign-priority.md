# Campaign research priority

**Status:** working priority after Keyword Planner research
**Date:** 2026-08-01

## Order

1. **Labels, stickers and product labels — Ukraine**
   - strongest balance of repeat B2B demand, forecast traffic and CPC;
   - dedicated landing page is required before launch.

2. **Business cards, leaflets and flyers — Ukraine**
   - strong search volume and relatively low forecast CPC;
   - page and margin review are required.

3. **Badges, branded lanyards and retractors — Ukraine**
   - clear product intent and repeat-order potential;
   - separate badge, lanyard and retractor groups.

4. **Window branding and working-hours signs — Kyiv**
   - local service with confirmed commercial demand;
   - final corrected Kyiv forecast still needs capture.

5. **Signs, lightboxes, dimensional letters and LED neon — Kyiv**
   - high-value direction with high CPC and competition;
   - LED/flexible neon is offered;
   - glass gas-discharge neon is not offered.

6. **Booklets, brochures and catalogues — Ukraine**
   - valid commercial demand but lower first-test priority.

7. **Printed menus — Ukraine**
   - valid demand but smaller clean volume and higher forecast CPC.

## Decision boundary

The order is not based only on modelled Google conversions. It also reflects
repeat-order value, operational fit, landing-page readiness, geography and
business priority.

No campaign is authorized by this document.
