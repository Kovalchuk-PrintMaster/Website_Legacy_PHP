## Landing pages required before launch

### Labels, stickers and product labels

Create one canonical commercial landing page that clearly covers:

- printed stickers and labels;
- labels for products;
- branded stickers;
- contour-cut stickers;
- material, size, quantity and finishing options;
- repeat B2B orders;
- delivery throughout Ukraine;
- a clear lead action.

### LED and flexible neon

Create or confirm a dedicated page for:

- LED neon;
- flexible neon;
- neon lettering and logos;
- design, fabrication and Kyiv installation.

Do not claim glass gas-discharge neon.

## URL rule

No campaign draft may use an inferred or guessed URL. Every final URL must be
copied from the production page and verified as HTTP 200 immediately before
campaign creation.
