# ForPrint Google Business Profile workspace

**Operational name:** `ForPrint_Google_Profile`
**Repository path:** `seo/google-business-profile/forprint/`
**Status:** preparation only; not yet published as a ForPrint Google Business Profile

## Purpose

This directory is the controlled preparation workspace for the future ForPrint
Google Business Profile.

It is not the website media library and it is not a production upload
directory. Raw photos and videos remain local working material until a separate
review approves them for publication.

## What belongs in the workspace root

Keep only the control files in this directory root:

```text
README.md
profile-data.md
media-manifest.csv
.gitignore
```

Do not place loose photographs or videos in the root.

## What to collect

```text
01-logo/                   approved logo candidates
02-cover/                  horizontal cover candidates
03-entrance-and-sign/      entrance, street context and permanent ForPrint sign
04-production/             production space and work process
05-equipment/              relevant equipment used for ForPrint services
06-team-at-work/           staff performing real work
07-finished-products/      10–15 strong real examples
08-profile-texts/          approved description, posts and response templates
09-services/               verified service list and factual notes
10-verification-evidence/  evidence needed for Google verification or support
```

## Working rules

1. Use factual materials only.
2. Do not mix Print Master branding into the ForPrint publication set.
3. Do not publish the ForPrint name in Google before the permanent sign and
   transition decision are ready.
4. Record every candidate file in `media-manifest.csv`.
5. Do not commit raw image/video files by default.
6. Approved final media may be versioned only through a separate controlled
   decision.
7. Never store passwords, account recovery data, private messages, tokens,
   identity documents or customer personal data here.

## Current transition rule

```text
Print Master remains an active equipment and service business.
Друкарня Smile remains the active print-shop profile for now.
ForPrint materials are prepared offline in this workspace.
After a permanent ForPrint sign exists, request a controlled
Smile → ForPrint transition decision from Google Business Profile support.
Do not create a duplicate ForPrint profile before that decision.
```

## Completion checklist

The workspace is ready for transition review when:

- the logo and horizontal cover are approved;
- the permanent ForPrint sign is installed and photographed;
- the entrance and street context are photographed;
- production, equipment and staff-at-work photos are selected;
- 10–15 finished-product examples are selected;
- `profile-data.md` contains confirmed facts;
- the service list contains no invented capabilities;
- the media manifest has a status for every candidate;
- Print Master and ForPrint contact/business identities remain separated.
