## 1. Decision

ForPrint does not create a new Google Business Profile while a same-location
print-shop profile already exists and the permanent ForPrint sign is not ready.

The operating model is:

```text
Print Master
→ active equipment, components, consumables and technical-service business

Друкарня Smile
→ active print-shop profile during the preparation period

ForPrint
→ future print and advertising-services identity prepared offline
```

The two active businesses share a location and management but have different
commercial roles and contact identities.

## 4. Transition prerequisites

Before requesting the transition:

1. install a permanent ForPrint sign;
2. photograph the sign, entrance, street context and production;
3. approve the ForPrint logo and horizontal cover;
4. collect 10–15 strong finished-product examples;
5. confirm business name, categories, phone, website, address and hours;
6. confirm the service list from actual production capabilities;
7. ensure Print Master and ForPrint remain commercially distinguishable;
8. prepare verification evidence without customer personal data.

## 5. Controlled transition

After the prerequisites are ready:

```text
review workspace
→ photograph permanent sign
→ make ForPrint identity consistent
→ contact Google Business Profile support
→ request guidance for Smile → ForPrint
→ follow the confirmed rebrand or new-profile procedure
→ verify
→ review public profile
```

Do not create a duplicate ForPrint profile in parallel.

## 6. Search-growth relationship

This local-profile preparation does not block:

- technical SEO;
- Search Console verification after DNS TXT is ready;
- Google Ads and Keyword Planner preparation;
- the 14-page initial launch package;
- labels landing-page preparation;
- website content work.

## 7. Evidence and privacy

Never commit:

- Google account passwords;
- recovery codes;
- private support conversations containing personal data;
- identity documents;
- customer contact data;
- tracking IDs or tokens unless separately approved as public configuration.

Screenshots used as evidence must be reviewed before versioning.
