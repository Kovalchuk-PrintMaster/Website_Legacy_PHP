# Дорожня карта пошуку, аналітики та Google Ads v0.1

**ID:** `FP-WEB-SEO-ROADMAP-001`
**Дата:** 2026-07-29
**Статус:** planned

## Фази

```text
0. HTTPS, canonical, crawl/index foundation
1. Consent-aware analytics and conversion measurement
2. Technical and multilingual search quality
3. Useful category/service/landing-page content
4. Small controlled Google Ads pilot
5. Reproducible Python analytics
6. Continuous measured optimization
```

Paid traffic starts only after HTTPS, destination quality, account readiness,
consent and conversion acceptance.

No ranking, traffic or revenue outcome is promised. Every change uses a
written hypothesis, baseline, primary metric, guardrails and result.

The roadmap is executed in two parallel tracks:

1. minimum viable promotion using verified existing pages;
2. structural and content expansion driven by real query and lead evidence.

Business prioritization favours repeat orders from small and medium businesses.
Local installation services start with Kyiv. Shippable products may expand
throughout Ukraine only from measured evidence.
