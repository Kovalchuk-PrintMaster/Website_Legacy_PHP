# Conditional negative-keyword decisions

These phrases are not automatically active across every plan.

## Badges

Add metal-badge phrases as negatives only when ForPrint does not manufacture or
supply metal badges.

## Window films

Add sun-protection, mirror and heat-reflective film phrases only when those
architectural-film services are not offered.

## Menus

Add QR-menu phrases only when ForPrint does not design or supply QR/digital
menus.

## Stickers

Add consumer decorative sticker phrases only when the campaign intentionally
excludes small retail packs.

## Catalogues

Add electronic/online catalogue phrases only when ForPrint does not provide
digital catalogue design.

A conditional phrase becomes active only after the commercial capability is
confirmed.
